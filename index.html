HOW A PROJECT RUNS

Same four stages, every project.

Assess

Site survey, process review and control philosophy discussion with your operations team.

Design

Single-line diagrams, panel GA drawings and application software specification, reviewed with you before build.

Build & test

Panels fabricated and factory-acceptance tested in-house; software staged and validated before shipping.

Commission & support

On-site installation, loop checks, commissioning, and handover into an ongoing AMC if required.
